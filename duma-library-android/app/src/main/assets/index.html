About DUMA Library

Browse the African way.

DUMA Online Library is being built to connect readers with African knowledge, local languages, education and literature from around the world.

Our first release links readers to open collections. A verified DUMA-hosted catalogue, reader accounts, author publishing, payments and audio licensing will be added after the secure publishing and rights systems are connected.
